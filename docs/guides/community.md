---
title: Votes, comments, flags, and co-maintainers
summary: What people can do on a package page.
audience: user
order: 50
---

Sign in and open a package.

- **Vote** once. Remove the vote from the same button. Popularity uses those votes and fades over time.
- **Flag out-of-date** with a short reason. The maintainer, a co-maintainer, or the person who flagged can clear it.
- **Comment** under the package. Edit your own comment. The maintainer, a co-maintainer, or a site admin can pin, unpin, or delete one.
- **Notifications** email you about updates and comments. Turn them off from the same page.
- **Co-maintainers** are set by the maintainer. The field replaces the whole list. An empty list clears it.

`taurctl pkg vote`, `flag`, `comment`, and `comaintainers` call the same API.
